ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making Lost Things Found Again.

CampusLeuth is a campus-focused Lost & Found platform designed to help students reconnect with their misplaced belongings.

Losing personal belongings on campus can be stressful. CampusLeuth makes the process easier by allowing students to report lost or found items, upload photographs, and search existing reports in one place. It aims to complement the existing physical Lost & Found facility with a convenient digital platform.
